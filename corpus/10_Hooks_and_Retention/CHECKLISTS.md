# QA checklist

- [ ] **shot lengths (hook join)**: check_shots

- Font legibility issues such as 0 vs O.
- Layout churn and pacing feel.

# Red flags

- More than ~0.3 s of silence at the start
- Hook longer than two sentences
- An ad-lib right after the hook

## Matching anti-patterns

- VDI-10-A01 Lead breath: Start on the syllable.
- VDI-10-A02 Undercut hook: Cut it if the join is clean.
