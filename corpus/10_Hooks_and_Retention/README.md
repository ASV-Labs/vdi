# 10_Hooks_and_Retention — Hooks and retention

What the first seconds must do, how platforms measure whether viewers stay, and the hook and pacing practice that AX used, with the honest gap that nothing on AX has been posted and measured yet.

Stages: script, edit.

## What this topic covers

- First 3-6 seconds
- Starting on the first syllable
- Punch-ins and payoff words
- Early screen beats
- Retention metrics

## Related topics

`01_Scripting_and_Boards`, `05_Editing_and_Pacing`, `09_Platform_Formats`

# Overview

## The short version

- **Lead with the proposition.** TikTok's ads guidance: put the hook in the first 6 seconds and introduce the proposition in the first 3. [SRC tiktok-creative]
- **Platforms measure the first seconds.** YouTube reports 'Stayed to watch' for Shorts: the share of times viewers stayed past the initial seconds; engaged views exclude loops. [SRC yt-analytics]
- **No retention data from AX yet.** None of the AX shorts has been posted; every hook rule here is from platform guidance or reviewer opinion, not from measured watch time. [AX lessons-001: 7 open questions]
