# 02_Talking_Head_Capture — agent rules

## Always

- **VDI-02-R01** · Always · stage: shoot — Use a phone rear camera at 4K held vertical, or a real camera; not a landscape webcam that must be cropped to 9:16. Check: Source resolution covers 1080x1920 without upscaling more than ~1.1x. [AX lessons-001: 2.1] [AX ax-editnotes: v4 item 1]
- **VDI-02-R02** · Always · stage: shoot — Put the key light in front of the face at about 45 degrees, at eye level; avoid overhead light. Check: No deep eye-socket shadows in a test frame. [AX ax-gr1: overhead light, eye-socket shadows] [AX lessons-001: 2.1] Angle [UNVERIFIED]
- **VDI-02-R03** · Always · stage: shoot — Frame the face for the 9:16 crop at capture: eye line about a third from the top, little empty headroom. Check: Test frame cropped to 9:16 needs no reframing. [AX ax-gr1: too wide, too much headroom]
- **VDI-02-R04** · Always · stage: shoot — Mic close: lav, or a phone within about 30 cm. Check: Floor between words at least 25-30 dB below speech in the raw take. [AX lessons-001: 2.2] Distance and floor target [UNVERIFIED]
- **VDI-02-R05** · Always · stage: shoot — Damp the room: soft furnishings or a blanket behind the camera. Check: Listen for room tail after a clap; compare raw floor to speech. [AX lessons-001: 2.2]
- **VDI-02-R06** · Always · stage: shoot — Finish the last word fully, then hold still and silent for one second. Check: Envelope of the raw take shows the final consonant and then room tone for >= 1 s. [AX lessons-001: 2.5] [AX ax-v5: T1]
- **VDI-02-R07** · Always · stage: shoot — Say each line twice with a pause. Check: Two reads per line in the raw take. [AX lessons-001: 2.6]
- **VDI-02-R08** · Always · stage: shoot — Note wardrobe, framing marks, light state and mic for every take, so pickups can match. Check: A capture sheet exists per short. [AX lessons-001: 2.3]

## Never

- **VDI-02-R09** · Never · stage: edit — Never try to rescue a soft webcam face with heavy sharpening or upscaling. Check: No unsharp/AI upscale stage on the face path beyond a light contrast-adaptive sharpen. [AX ax-editnotes: v4 item 1] [AX ax-gr4: #5 plastic face, halo]
