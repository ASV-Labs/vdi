# 03_Pickups_and_Continuity — Pickups and continuity

How to record and cut in replacement lines so the viewer cannot hear or see the join: same spot, light, wardrobe, framing and mic, and how to check it on the rendered file.

Stages: pickups.

## What this topic covers

- On-camera vs voice-only pickups
- Matching picture: spot, framing, light, wardrobe
- Matching sound: mic, EQ, level, room tone
- Where to place a join
- Checking joins on the final

## Related topics

`02_Talking_Head_Capture`, `07_Audio`, `08_Colour_and_Face`, `05_Editing_and_Pacing`

# Overview

## The short version

- **A pickup is part of the take, not a patch.** Pickups shot later were darker, warmer, softer and framed against a different part of the wall: skin dE 6.3-9.6 at the cut vs 0.2-3.1 within a take. [AX ax-v5: T3] [AX ax-reviewer: face grade]
- **Voice memos are a different microphone.** Phone voice memos sounded closer and drier than the webcam mic; joins needed EQ, level and room-tone matching. [AX ax-pickups: memo mic note] [AX lessons-001: 2.4]
- **Joins are checked on the final, with numbers.** The v4 gate said the largest level step was 1.3 LU; the independent reviewer measured 2.0-3.2 dB 1 s speech-level steps at 11 joins. Measure the rendered file, by a method that does not share the editor's assumptions. [AX ax-reviewer: level step] [AX ax-editnotes: v4 gate table]
