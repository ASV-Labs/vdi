# 03_Pickups_and_Continuity — agent rules

## Always

- **VDI-03-R01** · Always · stage: pickups — Shoot on-camera pickups at the same spot and framing (mark the tripod), same light (close curtains or same time of day), same shirt, same mic; same day if possible. Check: Skin dE < 3.5 at every take<->pickup cut on the final. [AX lessons-001: 2.3] [AX ax-v5: T3]
- **VDI-03-R02** · Always · stage: pickups — Record voice-only pickups on the same mic and spot as the take. Check: 1 s speech level across the join within 1 LU; no presence jump by ear. [AX lessons-001: 2.4]
- **VDI-03-R03** · Always · stage: edit — If the mic differs, match EQ (long-term spectrum on speech frames) and short-term level, and keep room tone running through the join. Check: Gate: 1 s level step < 2 dB; no digital-zero dropouts. [AX ax-editnotes: pickups section] [AX ax-v5: AX-05 15.83 s, 24.10 s]
- **VDI-03-R04** · Always · stage: edit — Grade each on-camera pickup to the take's skin lightness and colour, and match sharpness, before composing. Check: Gate: skin dE < 3.5 at the cut. [AX ax-v5: T3]
- **VDI-03-R05** · Always · stage: pickups — Read each pickup line twice and include a word or two of lead-in, so the join can sit in a natural pause. Check: Pickup files have two reads. [AX ax-pickups: lines read once] [AX lessons-001: 2.6]

## Never

- **VDI-03-R06** · Never · stage: edit — Never assume 'same camera, same spot' from a note; check the frames. Check: Compare background and skin across the cut on the final. [AX ax-reviewer: PICKUPS.md claimed same spot; backgrounds differ]

## Prefer

- **VDI-03-R07** · Prefer · stage: edit — Prefer to place a voice-only pickup under screen footage, so picture continuity is not at stake. Check: Voice pickups land in SCREEN beats. [AX ax-gr1: pickups table] [AX ax-pickups: memo note]
- **VDI-03-R08** · Prefer · stage: edit — Prefer the original take's words where they are clean, using the pickup only for the missing part (AX-01: 'then just ask in plain' from the take, 'English' from the pickup). Check: Re-transcribe the join with both models. [AX ax-v5: AX-01 27.5 s]
